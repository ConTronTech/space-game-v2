DESIGN (from docs/PHYSICS_RESEARCH.md and docs/QUESTIONS.md #14, both already answered by the user - read PHYSICS_RESEARCH.md section 3 first, it is the spec's rationale): real full N-body gravity is too expensive for the target laptop and not needed for this game. Instead: the ship feels gravity from exactly ONE body at a time - whichever body's sphere of influence (SOI) it is inside, nested innermost first (a moon's SOI wins over its planet's, which wins over the sun's). Planets/moons/the sun keep their existing free, driftless analytic orbits (world/star_system) UNCHANGED - nothing pulls on them, ever. This is the Kerbal Space Program "patched conic" approach: cheap, well understood, and it makes ship/orbit_lock's existing guide into real physics (fly tangentially at the guide's speed and gravity now actually curves you onto that orbit).
